## Units of Analysis
The artifact distinguishes between two units of analysis:
- **Papers**: used for corpus identification, screening, inclusion, and exclusion.
- **Systems**: used for analytic coding of scientific AI systems and workflows.

A single paper can and may describe more than one system.

## Coding Framework
Systems are coded according to:
- **Epistemic mode**
- **Primary workflow stage**
- **α (articulation work)**
- **γ (common ground)**
- **ω (awareness)**
- **Evaluation target**
- **Failure risk**

The α, γ, and ω dimensions use ordinal values from 0–3. Full operational definitions are provided in our paper (Table 4) and some in `03_Codebook.csv`.

## Methodological Scope
The corpus construction procedure uses a PRISMA-inspired reporting structure to improve transparency. The study is a focused conceptual survey rather than a fully systematic review and therefore does not claim strict PRISMA compliance.
